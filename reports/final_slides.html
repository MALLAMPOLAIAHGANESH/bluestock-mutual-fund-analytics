Bluestock Mutual Fund Capstone

End-to-End Analytics & Portfolio Optimisation

MALLAM POLAIAH GANESH
Data Analyst Intern, Bluestock Fintech

Project Objectives

  • Build a robust ETL pipeline for 10 mutual fund datasets.
  • Design an interactive Streamlit dashboard.
  • Calculate advanced risk metrics (VaR, CVaR, Sharpe, HHI).
  • Perform Monte Carlo NAV simulations.
  • Optimise portfolios using Markowitz Efficient Frontier.

Data Architecture & ETL

  • Source: 10 raw CSVs (Fund Master, NAV History, Inflows, etc.).
  • Transform: Handled NAV gaps (business day ffill), standardisation.
  • Load: Integrated into a 10-table SQLite database.
  • Scale: Processed over 46,000 NAV records and 32,000 transactions.

Interactive Dashboard

A comprehensive Streamlit app with 4 core views:

  1. Fund Overview: AUM and category distributions.
  2. Fund Deep Dive: NAV charts and Sharpe rankings.
  3. Investor Analysis: SIP trends and cohort behaviours.
  4. Risk Analytics: VaR, rolling Sharpe, and maximum drawdown.

Key Risk & Performance Insights

  • Top Performers: Mid/Small Cap funds dominate Sharpe ratio rankings.
  • Drawdowns: Monitored via maximum drawdown; Small Caps showed highest historical VaR.
  • Concentration: HHI scores identified heavily sector-concentrated portfolios.

Advanced Modelling: Monte Carlo

Simulated 10,000 paths over 5 years using Geometric Brownian Motion.

  • Small Caps: Widest confidence bands (highest risk/reward).
  • Large Caps: Tighter bands, more predictable compounding.

Advanced Modelling: Markowitz

Optimised a 5-fund portfolio.

  • Max Sharpe Portfolio: Heavily weighted towards high Alpha funds.
  • Min Variance Portfolio: Concentrated in stable Large Cap funds to minimise volatility.

Conclusion & Recommendations

  • Conservative Investors: Prioritise Large Cap funds (low VaR).
  • Aggressive Investors: Allocate to Mid/Small Cap (high Alpha).
  • Business Action: Focus SIP campaigns on Tier 2 cities showing high adoption growth.
